A statistician who learned to compete before she learned to code — and brings the same discipline to both.

**I study applied data science at the University of Chicago**, building on a B.S. in Statistics from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, where I graduated Magna Cum Laude with a 3.86 GPA. My work so far spans **financial research at Chicago Booth**, **agricultural survey statistics at the USDA**, and **live sports statistics for ESPN broadcasts** — three very different settings with the same core job: turn messy data into answers people can act on. Alongside the classroom, I captained a Division I soccer program and represent the **Czech Republic Women's National Team** at the youth international level.

## Experience highlights

**Researcher — University of Chicago Booth School of Business** (Summer 2025) — conducted market analysis to surface business trends and strategic opportunities across industries, and supported faculty research in healthcare finance by synthesizing data and analyzing financial models. Selected through the Leadership Alliance Summer Research Program; worked through the full research cycle, from framing a question to drawing conclusions.

**Survey Statistician Intern — USDA, National Agricultural Statistics Service** (Summer 2024, Des Moines, IA) — collected and analyzed Upper Midwest agricultural data in R and SAS to identify trends, and compiled reports published for internal and public use.

**Talent Statistician / Official Statistician — ESPN Statistics** (2023 – present) — assemble and interpret NCAA basketball, lacrosse, and volleyball data in real time and deliver live analytics to the broadcast team.

**Mathematics & Statistics Tutor — UMBC Academic Success Center** (2025) — explained complex mathematical and statistical concepts to strengthen student understanding.
